Weekly schedule

Getting stronger with light weights

  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then slow the lowering to 3 seconds.
  • Next, add a 1-second pause at the bottom of each rep.
  • Then move to single-leg versions and 20 reps.
  • Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. The timer starts when you tap a set.

Daily targets

  • About 2,800 kcal, 110 g protein, 3 L water.
  • Aim to gain 0.25–0.5 kg per week. If the scale hasn’t moved after 2 weeks, add 200 kcal.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours and weigh yourself weekly, in the morning.

Sample day

  • Breakfast: 3–4 eggs or paneer, oats with milk, banana
  • Lunch: rice, dal or chicken, vegetables, curd
  • Snack: peanut butter sandwich, glass of milk
  • Dinner: rotis or rice, chicken, fish, soya or paneer, vegetables
  • Before bed: milk and a handful of peanuts
